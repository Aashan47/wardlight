# Wardlight

Wardlight (working name) estimates, for each health facility, how many days a year its vaccine fridge stays
at 2 to 8 °C on the grid supply it is actually promised, and sizes the cheapest solar and battery system that
would keep it there.

Built for brief 1 ("Off-grid cold storage for clinics") of the IAS Global Energy Hackathon 2026, Digital
Solutions & AI track.

**Status: in development** for the IAS Global Energy Hackathon 2026.
