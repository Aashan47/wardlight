# Brief 13 sources: numbers for the success metrics

Research stream, written 31 Oct 2026. Scope: find and verify sources only; no code was changed.

## How this was checked

- Pages were downloaded headless with curl and stripped to text (or `pdftotext` for PDFs), then quoted from that text.
  Where a site refused curl (403), the archive.org copy was used and is named as such.
- Grades: **VERIFIED primary** = the publisher of the number, opened and quoted. **VERIFIED secondary** = a named
  outlet or document reporting someone else's number, opened and quoted. **UNVERIFIED** = only a search snippet or an
  unopenable page; the best candidate is given with the reason. **DERIVED** = arithmetic on verified rows, shown.
- Quotes are verbatim from the downloaded text. Line-wrap hyphenation and PDF column breaks were joined; nothing else
  was changed. Where a quote would carry an em dash it was cut short rather than retyped.

**Head range for the engine (DERIVED, labelled).** Upper aquifer motorised boreholes: static level about 16 m in
the 1960s (USGS) and about 20 m now (UNVERIFIED snippet), boreholes 60 to 70 m (UNVERIFIED snippet). With World
Bank's method (drawdown plus 10 to 15 m to an overhead tank plus losses), use **TDH 40 m point, range 30 to 70 m**
for upper aquifer boreholes. Lower aquifer ("ruwan zafi") boreholes are sub-artesian to artesian, so their head is
set by the tank and losses more than depth; do not apply the upper-aquifer head to them. Pump efficiency **0.45 point,
0.40 to 0.60 range** (60% is the World Bank's best-point figure; real small sets run below it).

## What contradicts or changes our current assumptions

1. **Clinic generator L/kWh is higher than the placeholder.** 0.303 to 0.454 fits a 20 to 90 kVA set above half load.
   A 15 to 20 kVA clinic set at quarter load burns 0.63 to 0.73 L/kWh (Cummins). Raising the clinic point to 0.55
   raises diesel litres avoided for clinics by about 45% over the 0.378 point.
2. **MEPP is gas by road tanker, not oil.** OSM's `plant:source=oil` disagrees with the plant manager (gas tankers)
   and GEM (natural gas, diesel listed as a second fuel). Treat it as a tanker-fed gas turbine.
3. **MEPP delivered far below nameplate:** 14.20 MW daily average against 32 MW generating and a 50 MW upgrade
   (Dec 2024, NAN). Support per kWh is about N193 at that output, N86 at 32 MW, N55 at 50 MW (DERIVED).
4. **MEPP's own cost is 15.88 USc/kWh against 6.2 USc/kWh paid by NBET** (Sep 2024): the subsidy problem predates
   the N68bn.
5. **UNIMAID/UMTH carries 7 MWh of batteries and a 10 MW diesel backup** (resident engineer, May 2024). The UMTH
   share is 5 MW in one source and 4.5 MW in another.
6. **The NBS May 2026 diesel price is national.** Borno in May 2026 sits below N3,388.85 (outside the top 10); the
   North-East was not the top zone in May (North-West was). April 2026 Borno was N2,684.95.
7. **Maiduguri boreholes come in two kinds:** upper aquifer (shallower, pumped) and lower aquifer "ruwan zafi"
   (deep, about 40 years old, now 50 hp solar or hybrid). One head range does not fit both.
8. **The 2024 water crisis had a diesel-theft component:** fuel for 6 hours a day, 1 hour pumped. This supports the
   ward-committee ownership row and is a real argument for solar at boreholes.
