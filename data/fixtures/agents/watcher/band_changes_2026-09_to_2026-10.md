# Band changes: NERC cap orders 2026-09 to 2026-10

106 feeders listed in both months changed band (2290 listed in both; 5 added, 4 dropped). Source: naija-grid caps_long.csv, credit NERC.

67 of them link by name to a NERC row Wardlight reads.
3016 facilities are affected; 3016 were re-run.
- For 2959 of them Wardlight's data already shows the 2026-10 bands (the NERC row it reads carries the new band): the figures below say what the change did.
- 250 now need a PQS solar direct-drive fridge (before: a PQS mains ice-lined fridge).
- 28 now need a PQS mains ice-lined fridge (before: a PQS solar direct-drive fridge).
